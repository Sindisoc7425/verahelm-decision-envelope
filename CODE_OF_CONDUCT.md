# Code of conduct

Be precise, respectful, and evidence-oriented. Discuss technical claims without harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or disclosure of another person’s private information.

Do not post vulnerabilities, credentials, customer material, or confidential data. Report security concerns through the private channel in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit or remove material that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.
